Common Questions

Straight answers before you push or ride your bike over.

How long will my repair take?

Most flats, chain fixes, and basic tune-ups are finished the same day, usually within an hour or two. Larger jobs like gear overhauls or full rebuilds may stay overnight or for two days. We give you a clear time estimate at drop-off.

Do I need to book an appointment first?

No appointment is needed for everyday repairs. Walk right in between 8:00 AM and 5:30 PM. If you have an unusual tyre size or major job, calling ahead lets us set your parts aside.

What types and brands of bicycles do you fix?

We repair road, mountain, hybrid, commuter, BMX, and kids' bikes across all major brands. If your bike uses a rare component, send us a quick message or photo first.

Will you confirm the cost before starting work?

Always. We inspect your bike and call or WhatsApp you with the diagnosis and exact price before starting. Nothing happens without your go-ahead.
